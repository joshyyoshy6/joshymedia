about me

joshy is a los angeles-based creative director and photographer working across fashion, culture, and commercial imagery. his work brings together a considered visual approach with an instinct for creating imagery that feels distinct, intentional, and culturally relevant.

working with brands, musicians, and independent creatives, he moves between concept development, direction, and production, shaping visual identities and translating ideas into compelling visual work.
